Happy holidays!

It's December already. The year has gone by so fast and so slow at the same time. The first half of the year was just me kind of fucking around, I hardly remember anything, and then the second half of the year, I got really busy, the busiest I've been in life in a while. I heard this saying once, "the days are long but the years are short", and as I get older it really does feel that way. It's difficult for me to not feel like I am wasting my life away because of it, since I'm not where I'd like to be, but I could probably be in an even worse spot, so I can't complain.

I think it's easy for me to compare myself to other people my age (doesn't everyone?) and I'm always a little disappointed in myself, but that's not the way to do it. It's better to compare your current self to your past self since everyone has different circumstances in life. This year, when I think about it, I think I've improved a lot from last year.. My drawing skills aren't any different and I didn't really learn anything new, and I kind of stopped posting on social media (regrettably), but I held down a job, and started a side gig, and I'm learning to be more independent and financially literate everyday, so there's that, and I think I should be happy with myself for it. Also, I managed to keep this site up and running. Which is sick!

Now is a time to make new years resolutions isn't it? I stopped doing that a long time ago because after a while I found it to be pointless. Anyway, this year, my new years resolution is to save money to see my friends, and to draw and post more, and to be nicer to myself. That's kind of it.

Lately, I have been watching a lot of animal documentaries. I grew up watching a lot of animal documentaries on TV, and I think I started drawing because of that. I rememeber I'd only ever draw animals, and never humans, since animals are so much more interesting in shape and size and stuff.
